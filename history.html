Whalestory

The Secret Society of Whales launched in August 2021 as a collection of 10,000 ERC-721 whales minted at 0.05 ETH. What began as a mint with a roadmap of charity milestones grew into a community-governed vault, a token economy, a gaming guild, and a run of collections spanning Orcas, Wealthy Whales, Pixel Whales, and Collabs. This is the record of how it happened, month by month, drawn from the community's own announcements.

    How to play

    Shooting

    Drag anywhere on the table to swing the cue around; a quick tap aims straight at that spot. Use Fine aim for tiny turns. Then drag the power bar and let go to shoot (slide it back to the start to cancel). Spin sets where the cue hits the cue ball: low draws it back, high follows through, left and right bend it off the cushions.

    On a computer: arrow keys aim (hold Shift for big turns), W/S set power, Space shoots.

    Fouls

    Scratching (the cue ball drops), missing everything, hitting the wrong ball first, or nothing reaching a cushion after the hit. After a foul the other player gets ball in hand: drag the cue ball anywhere. The break is taken from behind the line.

    8-Ball

    The table is open after the break. Your first pot decides whether you’re on solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15). Clear yours, then tap a pocket and sink the 8 there. The 8 early, in the wrong pocket or with a scratch loses.

    9-Ball & 10-Ball

    Always hit the lowest ball first; anything that drops keeps you shooting. In 9-ball the 9 wins whenever it drops on a good shot. In 10-ball you call each shot — the aim line picks the ball and pocket, or tap them yourself — and only a called 10 wins.

    Online

    Each shot has a 45-second clock. Run out and it’s a foul: ball in hand to the other player. Run out three times in a row and you lose the rack. Close the page and the link brings you back to your seat.

    Every shot is refereed by the server, so what you see is what happened — for both of you.
